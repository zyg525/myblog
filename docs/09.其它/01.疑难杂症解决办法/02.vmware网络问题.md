## 一、网络重启报错问题

　　当使用`systemctl restart network`重启网络时，报错`Failed to start LSB: Bring`。

　　解决办法：禁用NetworkManager

　　1、`systemctl stop NetworkManager`

　　2、`systemctl disable NetworkManager`

　　3、重启网络，`systemctl restart network`
